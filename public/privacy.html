BabyClock Terms

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 13, 2026

Overview

BabyClock is a baby care reminder app designed to help parents create and manage reminders for feeding, diaper changes, sleep, medicine, appointments, and custom routines.

The core app is designed to store reminder data locally on your device. BabyClock does not require an account for basic reminder use.

Information stored by the app

BabyClock may store reminder titles, reminder types, schedule times, repeat settings, notes, notification preferences, and local reminder history on your device.

This information is used only to operate the reminder and journal features inside the app.

Subscriptions

BabyClock uses RevenueCat to manage subscriptions and entitlement status for BabyClock Pro. RevenueCat and the app store may process purchase identifiers, subscription status, receipts, and related transaction information needed to manage purchases.

Notifications

BabyClock uses local notifications to remind you about scheduled baby care routines. Notification permissions are controlled by your device settings.

Data sharing

BabyClock does not sell personal data. Core reminder data is not sent to a BabyClock backend. Subscription processing is handled by the app store and RevenueCat.

Children's privacy

BabyClock is intended for use by parents and caregivers. It is not intended to be used directly by children.

Medical disclaimer

BabyClock is a reminder utility and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Always consult a qualified healthcare professional for medical concerns.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the BabyClock developer through the support contact listed on the Google Play Store page.
